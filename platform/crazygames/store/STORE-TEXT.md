## Title

Capybara with a Gun

## Short description (one line)

The ants never stop coming. Your capybara's gun never stops firing. Survive.

## Description

Ant monsters are pouring out of the ground, and there's only one capybara standing in their way. Your gun fires by itself at the nearest ant. Your job is to keep moving, grab the seeds, level up and stay alive.

Every level-up gives you a choice. Pick a weapon path at your first level: Gunner, Pistolier, Shotgunner, Sniper, Pyro or Bombardier. Each path has its own upgrades, and maxing one out evolves your gun into something much bigger. From the second area you get a heavy weapon too, firing alongside your gun: a Tesla Coil, Missile Pod, Saw Launcher or Frost Cannon. At level 10 a sidekick joins in.

Every two minutes, something big shows up. There are 15 bosses across three areas, each with its own entrance and attacks:

- **The Meadow:** the Queen, Big Chompy, Talon, Slithers and the ANT-1000.
- **The Scorched Dunes:** the Pharaoh, a giant scorpion, a sandworm, an armadillo and the Iron Sphinx.
- **The Midnight Swamp:** Mother Leech, the Bog King, Mama Skeeter, King Fungus and Octo-Tron.

Clear all three areas to reach the Gauntlet, an endless run of laps mixing every area's creatures and bosses.

**Features**

- Six weapon paths, each with its own evolution, plus four heavy weapons and two sidekicks
- 15 bosses that scale with your build and get angry at half health
- Elite ants that drop treasure chests, map power-ups (seed magnet, freeze bomb, rage chilli) and hazards
- Easy, Normal and Nightmare difficulty
- A daily challenge and a weekly challenge, each with its own twist
- Rerolls and banishes on the level-up screen, with more to buy in the shop using coins you earn in runs
- A wardrobe of capybara skins and hats to unlock, a bestiary, achievements and lifetime stats
- Plays with keyboard, touch or controller
- Progress saves to your CrazyGames account

## Submission settings to remember

- Turn on **Progress Save**, so saves go through the SDK data module.
- **Exclusivity:** leave it off. The game is also on petrieuk.co.uk.
- **PEGI:** cartoon violence against fantasy creatures (ants, bugs, robots). No blood, people or real-world weapons in close-up. This should sit within PEGI 7–12. Don't market it at children, because CrazyGames rejects games aimed at kids.
- **Languages:** English only.
