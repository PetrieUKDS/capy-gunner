The ant monsters are coming out of the ground and they never stop. Your gun fires by itself at the closest ant. Keep moving, grab the green seeds to level up, and call in the chopper when the meter is full. At your first level-up you choose a path: Gunner, Pistolier, Shotgunner, Sniper, Pyro or Bombardier. Every two minutes, something big shows up.

MoveWASD or arrow keys. On a phone, drag anywhere.
AirstrikeSpace, or tap the big round button when it glows.
Gunner camPress C for heat-vision mode.
PauseP or Esc.
ControllerLeft stick or d-pad to move, A for the airstrike, Start to pause. Menus work with the stick and A.
LootGlowing elite ants drop treasure chests. Grab magnets, freeze bombs and chillies too.
